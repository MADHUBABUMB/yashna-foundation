Who we are

Compassion is where change begins.

Yashna Foundation Telangana is built around a simple belief: every life matters. By bringing volunteers and caring people together, we aim to turn kindness into practical action for people, animals, and the natural world.

Whether it is helping someone find a blood donor, sharing a meal, planting a tree, or helping an animal in distress, every responsible action counts.

Connect with our team

Our mission

To encourage a culture of service, empathy, and environmental responsibility—one person, one community, and one act of kindness at a time.
